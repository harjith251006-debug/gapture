## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy sets out the operational and control framework governing client order receipt, order management, trade execution, and client asset protection at PrimeAxis Broking Services Pvt. Ltd. ("PrimeAxis" or "the Company"), a securities intermediary registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India. It applies to all dealing, operations, and risk personnel and to all client segments serviced by the Company.

## 2. Client Order Receipt

2.1 Client orders shall be accepted only through approved channels: the Company's trading application, its call-and-trade desk, or, for institutional clients, through approved order management system (OMS) connectivity.

2.2 Orders received through the call-and-trade desk shall be recorded through the Company's telephone recording system at the point of receipt, and the recording shall be linked to the corresponding order in the trading system.

2.3 Walk-in orders shall not be accepted; all clients shall place orders through one of the approved channels listed in Section 2.1.

## 3. Order Authentication

3.1 Orders placed through the trading application shall be authenticated through the client's unique login credentials and, where applicable, a second-factor authentication mechanism.

3.2 Orders placed through the call-and-trade desk shall be authenticated by verifying the client's registered mobile number and a pre-set telephone identification PIN.

3.3 Dealers shall log in to the dealing terminal using their individually assigned credentials; shared or generic dealer logins are not permitted under normal circumstances. During periods of dealer absence due to sudden illness or system access failure, a supervisor may temporarily authorize use of a designated backup terminal under the supervisor's own credentials, with the substitute dealer's identity recorded in the shift log for that session.

## 4. Order Recording

4.1 Every client order shall be recorded in the Order Management System with a unique order ID, time stamp, client ID, instrument, quantity, price/type, and receiving dealer's identity.

4.2 Order records shall be immutable once entered; any change to an order shall be captured as a new event (modification or cancellation) rather than an overwrite of the original entry.

## 5. Order Execution

5.1 Orders shall be routed to the exchange in the sequence received, subject to applicable order type and market conditions, and executed in accordance with exchange rules.

5.2 Dealers shall not execute orders on their own account ahead of client orders in the same security (front-running is strictly prohibited).

5.3 Execution details (price, quantity, exchange order number, and time of execution) shall be captured against the corresponding client order automatically by the OMS upon exchange confirmation.

## 6. Client Instructions

6.1 All client instructions relating to order placement, modification, or cancellation shall be given through an approved channel as described in Section 2.

6.2 Standing instructions (such as recurring systematic investment orders) shall be documented and stored against the client's profile, and shall be reviewed with the client at defined intervals.

## 7. Order Modification and Cancellation

7.1 Clients may modify or cancel unexecuted orders through the same channels used for order placement, subject to exchange rules on modification.

7.2 Manual modification of a client's order by a dealer, on the dealer's own initiative rather than at the client's instruction, shall not ordinarily be permitted. Where a dealer identifies an evident input error (such as an incorrect quantity or price that is disproportionate to the client's typical order size) and is unable to reach the client promptly, the dealer may cancel the erroneous order and place a corrected order at the client's last confirmed instruction, recording the rationale in the dealer's shift notes for supervisory review at the end of the trading day.

## 8. Dealer Responsibilities

8.1 Dealers shall execute client orders strictly in accordance with client instructions and applicable exchange rules.

8.2 Dealers shall not accept orders from any person other than the client or the client's authorized representative on record.

8.3 Dealers shall immediately report any suspected unauthorized order, system malfunction, or unusual market activity to the shift supervisor.

## 9. Client Funds

9.1 Client funds shall be maintained in a segregated client bank account, distinct from the Company's own funds, in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

9.2 Client funds shall not be used to meet the Company's own obligations or the obligations of any other client.

9.3 Running account settlement with clients, where opted by the client, shall be carried out at the intervals and in the manner prescribed under the client's running account authorization.

## 10. Client Securities

10.1 Client securities shall be held in the client's own demat account or in a designated client securities account, and shall not be commingled with the Company's proprietary securities holdings.

10.2 Pledging or use of client securities for any purpose shall require explicit client authorization in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and the Company's margin pledge procedures.

## 11. Margin Requirements

11.1 Clients shall maintain margins as prescribed by exchange and regulatory requirements for the relevant product segment prior to order placement.

11.2 The Risk team shall monitor margin utilization on a real-time basis and shall trigger margin call notifications and, where necessary, square-off of positions in accordance with the Company's Risk Management Policy.

### Table 1: Illustrative Margin Shortfall Workflow

| Stage | Trigger | Action | Responsible Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alert | Margin utilization exceeds 80% | Client notification (SMS/email/app) | Risk |
| Margin Call | Margin utilization exceeds 90% | Formal margin call communication | Risk |
| Square-off | Shortfall not met by cut-off time | Automated square-off of positions | Risk Systems |
| Post-square-off review | Square-off executed | Reconciliation and client communication | Operations |

## 12. Risk Controls

12.1 Pre-trade risk controls, including exposure limits, price bands, and quantity freezes, shall be configured in the OMS and applied to all client and proprietary orders.

12.2 Risk limit overrides shall require approval from a designated Risk Officer and shall be logged with the reason for the override.

12.3 In exceptional market conditions requiring urgent risk mitigation (such as extreme volatility events), the Head of Risk may authorize a temporary, system-wide adjustment to standard risk parameters, with such adjustment reported to the Risk Committee at its next scheduled meeting.

## 13. Unauthorized Trading Prevention

13.1 Access to the dealing terminal and OMS shall be restricted to authorized dealing personnel through individual login credentials, as described in Section 3.3.

13.2 Any trade identified as unauthorized shall be reported to Compliance and Risk immediately upon discovery, and shall be subject to the Company's error account and client redressal procedures.

13.3 Surveillance shall periodically review dealer trading patterns to identify potential unauthorized trading, self-dealing, or client account misuse.

## 14. Trade Confirmations

14.1 Trade confirmations shall be sent to clients following execution, via SMS and/or email, confirming the security, quantity, price, and order/trade reference.

14.2 Confirmations should be dispatched as soon as reasonably practicable following execution; during periods of high market volume, dispatch may be batched and sent within the same trading day rather than immediately upon each individual execution.

## 15. Contract Notes

15.1 Contract notes shall be issued to clients for all executed trades in accordance with applicable regulatory timelines and format requirements.

15.2 Contract notes shall be digitally signed and dispatched to the client's registered email address, with physical dispatch available upon client request.

## 16. Complaints

16.1 Client complaints relating to order execution, unauthorized trading, or contract note discrepancies shall be logged in the Company's grievance register and routed to the relevant department for resolution.

16.2 Complaints shall be acknowledged and resolved in accordance with the Company's Investor Grievance Redressal Policy.

## 17. Surveillance

17.1 The Surveillance team shall monitor trading activity for patterns indicative of market manipulation, circular trading, front-running, and other prohibited practices, using exchange alerts and internal surveillance tools.

17.2 Surveillance alerts shall be reviewed, dispositioned, and, where warranted, escalated to Compliance for further investigation and potential regulatory reporting.

## 18. Audit Trails

18.1 The OMS and dealing terminal shall maintain a complete audit trail of order entry, modification, cancellation, and execution events, including the identity of the acting dealer or client channel and the associated time stamp.

18.2 Telephone recordings of call-and-trade orders shall be retained and linked to the corresponding order record for the retention period specified in Section 20.

18.3 Audit trail completeness for orders modified under the exception process described in Section 7.2 depends on the dealer's shift notes being accurately transcribed into the system; where a dealer's end-of-day notes are incomplete, the corresponding audit trail for that modification may not fully capture the rationale for the change.

## 19. Employee Access

19.1 Access to client order data, OMS functions, and dealing terminals shall be granted on a role-appropriate basis and reviewed periodically by IT and Compliance.

19.2 Departing employees' access shall be revoked promptly upon cessation of employment.

## 20. Segregation of Duties

20.1 The functions of order execution, trade confirmation, contract note generation, and client fund reconciliation shall be performed by separate teams to the extent practicable.

20.2 Dealers shall not have access to modify client fund ledgers or contract note records.

## 21. Regulatory Reporting

21.1 The Company shall submit periodic trading and risk-related reports to the relevant stock exchanges and regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable requirements.

21.2 Compliance shall maintain a reporting calendar and shall be responsible for timely submission of all applicable reports.

## 22. Policy Exceptions

22.1 Exceptions to this Policy shall be documented and approved by the Head of Trading Operations or the Chief Compliance Officer, as appropriate to the nature of the exception, and reported to the Board Compliance & Risk Committee.
