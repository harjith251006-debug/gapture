## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy sets out Artha Financial Services Ltd.'s ("Artha" or "the Company") framework for the onboarding, risk assessment, monitoring, and termination of outsourcing and vendor relationships, including subcontracting arrangements. It applies to all business units engaging third-party vendors in connection with the Company's regulated activities.

## 2. Vendor Onboarding

**VR-01.** All prospective vendors providing services material to the Company's operations shall be onboarded through the centralized Vendor Management System ("VMS") following completion of due diligence described in Section 3.

**VR-02.** Business units shall not engage a vendor for material services without prior approval from the Vendor Risk Management team.

**VR-03.** In situations requiring urgent engagement of a vendor to address an operational disruption (for example, a failure of an existing critical service provider), the business unit head may authorize provisional onboarding ahead of completion of full due diligence, subject to a lightweight interim risk check, with full due diligence to be completed within a reasonable period following provisional onboarding.

**VR-04.** Provisional onboarding under Section 2's emergency exception shall be reported to the Vendor Risk Management team, which shall track such vendors until full due diligence is completed; no fixed maximum duration for provisional status is specified in this Policy.

## 3. Due Diligence

**VR-05.** Due diligence shall assess the vendor's financial stability, operational capability, information security posture, regulatory standing, and reputational history.

**VR-06.** Due diligence shall be proportionate to the criticality classification assigned to the vendor under Section 4.

**VR-07.** Vendors providing services involving access to customer data or core systems shall additionally undergo an information security assessment coordinated with the Information Security team.

**VR-08.** Due diligence findings shall be documented and retained in the VMS for the duration of the vendor relationship.

## 4. Risk Classification

**VR-09.** Every vendor shall be assigned a risk classification — Low, Medium, High, or Critical — based on the nature of services provided, data access, and potential impact of service failure.

**VR-10.** Risk classification shall be reviewed at defined intervals, and additionally upon material change in the scope of services provided by the vendor.

### Table 1: Vendor Risk Classification

| Classification | Criteria | Review Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Low | No access to customer data; non-critical support services | Every 3 years |
| Medium | Limited data access; moderate operational dependency | Every 2 years |
| High | Significant data access or operational dependency | Annually |
| Critical | Core system support, customer data processing, or business-critical function | Annually, with interim review as needed |

## 5. Critical Vendors

**VR-11.** Vendors classified as Critical shall be subject to enhanced oversight, including a designated relationship owner within the Company, more frequent performance reviews, and inclusion in the Company's business continuity planning.

**VR-12.** The Board Outsourcing Committee shall maintain visibility of all Critical vendor relationships and shall review this list at least annually.

## 6. Outsourcing Agreements

**VR-13.** All outsourcing arrangements shall be governed by a written agreement setting out scope of services, service levels, data protection obligations, audit rights, and termination provisions.

**VR-14.** Outsourcing agreements for Critical and High-risk vendors shall be reviewed by Legal and Compliance prior to execution.

**VR-15.** Standard agreement templates shall include a clause obligating the vendor to comply with applicable laws and regulations relevant to the services provided; the specific regulatory requirements applicable to the vendor's role are, in the standard template, identified by the vendor itself as part of its compliance representation, rather than being independently enumerated by Artha in the agreement.

## 7. Confidentiality

**VR-16.** All vendor agreements shall include confidentiality obligations covering Company and customer information accessed or generated in the course of providing services.

**VR-17.** Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of the agreement for a period specified in the agreement, generally not less than three years.

## 8. Customer Data

**VR-18.** Vendors with access to customer data shall be contractually obligated to implement security controls consistent with the Company's Information Security Policy.

**VR-19.** Vendor access to customer data shall be limited to what is necessary for the vendor to perform the contracted services.

**VR-20.** Verification of a vendor's actual implementation of the security controls referenced in Section 8's data protection clause is generally based on the vendor's self-certification submitted annually, supplemented by an independent security assessment only for vendors classified as Critical.

## 9. Subcontractors

**VR-21.** Vendors shall not subcontract material elements of the contracted services without prior written approval from Artha.

**VR-22.** Approval of subcontracting arrangements shall be coordinated by the vendor relationship owner; the criteria for what constitutes a "material element" requiring approval, as opposed to a minor subcontracted component not requiring approval, is determined on a case-by-case basis by the relationship owner in consultation with Vendor Risk Management, without a standardized definition set out in this Policy.

**VR-23.** Where subcontracting is approved, the vendor remains fully responsible for the subcontractor's performance and compliance with the terms of the outsourcing agreement.

## 10. Audit Rights

**VR-24.** Outsourcing agreements for Critical and High-risk vendors shall include a right for Artha, or its nominated auditor, to audit the vendor's operations relevant to the services provided.

**VR-25.** Audit rights for Medium and Low-risk vendors are addressed through the standard agreement template's general cooperation clause, which requests the vendor's reasonable cooperation with Company inquiries, rather than through a dedicated on-site audit right.

**VR-26.** Exercise of audit rights for Critical vendors shall be planned as part of the annual vendor oversight calendar, subject to the vendor's reasonable scheduling availability.

## 11. Regulatory Access

**VR-27.** Outsourcing agreements for Critical and High-risk vendors should include a provision enabling regulatory authorities to access relevant information and inspect the vendor's premises in connection with the outsourced activity, to the extent required under applicable regulatory guidance.

**VR-28.** Where the standard regulatory access clause under Section 11 has not been included in a legacy agreement executed prior to the introduction of this provision, the vendor relationship owner should seek to incorporate the clause at the next contract renewal, rather than through a contract amendment.

## 12. Business Continuity

**VR-29.** Critical vendors shall be required to maintain a business continuity plan for the services provided, and to make relevant summary information available to Artha upon reasonable request.

**VR-30.** Artha's own business continuity planning shall account for the potential unavailability of Critical vendors, including identification of alternate arrangements where feasible.

## 13. Incident Reporting

**VR-31.** Vendors shall notify Artha of any security incident, data breach, or material service disruption affecting the services provided.

**VR-32.** The notification timeline for vendor-reported incidents is set out in individual outsourcing agreements and may vary by vendor; the standard template suggests notification "as soon as reasonably possible," and specific numeric timelines (e.g., 24 or 72 hours) are negotiated on a contract-by-contract basis rather than fixed centrally by this Policy.

## 14. Vendor Monitoring

**VR-33.** Vendor performance shall be monitored on an ongoing basis by the relationship owner, including service level adherence, incident history, and any customer or regulatory complaints linked to the vendor.

**VR-34.** Formal vendor performance reviews shall be conducted at the frequency specified in Table 1; in practice, review timeliness for Medium and Low-risk vendors has varied depending on relationship owner workload, and reviews falling behind schedule are addressed on a best-effort basis rather than through a formal escalation process.

## 15. Performance Review

**VR-35.** Performance reviews shall assess service quality, compliance with contractual obligations, and continued appropriateness of the vendor's risk classification.

**VR-36.** Performance issues identified during review shall be documented, communicated to the vendor, and tracked to resolution.

## 16. Termination

**VR-37.** Outsourcing agreements shall include termination provisions covering notice periods, transition assistance, and data return or deletion obligations.

**VR-38.** Termination for cause (e.g., material breach, security incident, regulatory concern) shall be escalated to the Vendor Risk Management team and, for Critical vendors, to the Board Outsourcing Committee.

## 17. Data Return / Deletion

**VR-39.** Upon termination of the vendor relationship, the vendor shall return or securely delete all Company and customer data in its possession, and shall certify completion of this process.

**VR-40.** Certification of data deletion shall be retained in the VMS; independent verification of the vendor's deletion certification (for example, through a technical audit) is performed only for Critical vendors and only where deemed warranted by the circumstances of the termination.

## 18. Record Retention

**VR-41.** Vendor due diligence records, agreements, performance reviews, and incident records shall be retained for the duration of the relationship and for a minimum of five years thereafter.

## 19. Management Oversight

**VR-42.** The Vendor Risk Management team shall present a consolidated vendor risk report to senior management on a periodic basis, covering onboarding volumes, risk classification distribution, overdue reviews, and material incidents.

**VR-43.** The Board Outsourcing Committee shall review the Critical vendor portfolio and any material outsourcing-related incidents at least annually.

## 20. Policy Exceptions

**VR-44.** Exceptions to this Policy shall be documented and approved by the Head of Vendor Risk Management, with exceptions involving Critical vendors additionally requiring notification to the Board Outsourcing Committee.

## 21. Document Review

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically to reflect changes in the Company's outsourcing footprint, vendor risk landscape, and applicable regulatory expectations.
