## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy governs the processing of inward and outward foreign remittances and other cross-border transactions undertaken by Orion Finserv Ltd. ("Orion" or "the Company"). It sets out operational requirements relating to customer verification, transaction approval, documentation, reporting, and record-keeping, and applies to all cross-border transaction activity conducted directly by Orion or through its authorized banking partners.

## 2. Inward Remittances

2.1 Inward foreign remittances shall be credited to the beneficiary's account only after the purpose of remittance has been captured and, where applicable, validated against supporting documentation.

2.2 Inward remittances below the threshold specified in Annexure A should be processed on a same-day basis, subject to satisfactory completion of screening checks.

2.3 Where the remitting bank is a correspondent of long standing with an established relationship with Orion, the purpose classification and screening performed by the correspondent bank may be relied upon, and Orion's processing team need not independently re-verify the underlying purpose documentation before crediting the beneficiary.

## 3. Outward Remittances

3.1 Outward remittance requests shall be accompanied by a duly completed application form, purpose declaration, and supporting documentation appropriate to the purpose category (e.g., invoice, education offer letter, medical estimate, investment agreement).

3.2 Outward remittances shall be processed only after verification of the remitter's identity, source of funds, and compliance with applicable limits.

3.3 Outward remittance requests exceeding the threshold specified in Annexure A shall require sign-off from a designated Compliance Officer prior to release of funds.

## 4. Non-Resident Customers

4.1 Non-resident customers shall be identified using their passport, overseas address proof, and, where applicable, visa or residency permit.

4.2 Remittance facilities extended to non-resident customers shall be subject to the same operational controls as resident customers, supplemented by verification of the customer's non-resident status and the applicable account category (NRE, NRO, or equivalent).

4.3 Where a non-resident customer's account was opened by a partner bank under a referral arrangement, Orion's onboarding team should obtain confirmation of KYC completion from the partner bank and may proceed with account activation on that basis, without independently repeating the underlying identity verification.

## 5. Foreign Currency Transactions

5.1 All foreign currency transactions shall be converted at rates published by Treasury, updated at intervals during the business day.

5.2 Foreign currency transaction records shall capture the currency pair, exchange rate applied, and equivalent domestic currency value for reporting purposes.

## 6. Purpose Classification

6.1 Every cross-border transaction shall be classified under an appropriate purpose code prior to processing.

6.2 Purpose classification shall be validated against the supporting documentation provided by the customer; where documentation is ambiguous, the processing officer should select the closest applicable purpose code and note the ambiguity in the transaction remarks field.

## 7. Customer Verification

7.1 Customer identity shall be verified at onboarding and periodically thereafter in accordance with the Company's KYC Policy.

7.2 For each cross-border transaction, the processing officer shall confirm that the customer's KYC is current and that the transaction is consistent with the customer's declared profile.

## 8. Beneficiary Verification

8.1 Beneficiary bank account and SWIFT/IBAN details shall be verified prior to the first remittance to a new beneficiary.

8.2 Requests to change a registered beneficiary's account details may be submitted by the customer via their registered email address, accompanied by the customer ID and a scanned copy of a supporting document (such as a bank letter). Such requests shall be actioned by the processing team upon receipt, and a confirmation notification shall be sent to the customer's registered email and mobile number after the change has been made.

8.3 Where a beneficiary change request appears unusual — for example, a change immediately preceding a large remittance — the processing officer should exercise additional caution and may seek telephonic confirmation with the customer, though this is not a mandatory step for all beneficiary changes under Section 8.2.

## 9. Authorized Banking Channels

9.1 Cross-border transactions shall be routed exclusively through banking partners and payment networks authorized under Orion's correspondent banking arrangements.

9.2 The list of authorized banking partners shall be maintained by Treasury and reviewed periodically for continued regulatory standing.

## 10. Transaction Approval

10.1 Cross-border transactions shall be approved through a maker-checker process, with the checker being independent of the maker, for all transactions above the retail threshold specified in Annexure A.

10.2 In emergency situations — including system outages, urgent customer commitments (such as tuition fee deadlines or medical emergencies), or checker unavailability — a designated supervisor may authorize release of funds ahead of completion of the standard checking process, with the checking review completed retrospectively as soon as practicable thereafter. Such retrospective approvals shall be logged in the exception register.

10.3 Retrospective approvals under Section 10.2 should be reviewed by Compliance in the ordinary course of its periodic monitoring activities.

## 11. Documentation

11.1 Supporting documentation for each cross-border transaction shall be retained along with the transaction record, including the application form, purpose documentation, and identity verification records applicable at the time of transaction.

11.2 Documentation may be retained in scanned electronic form, provided the original is available for inspection upon request for the period specified in Section 18.

## 12. FEMA-Related Operational Checks

12.1 Processing staff shall apply operational checks consistent with prevailing foreign exchange management requirements, including verification of permissible purpose, applicable limits, and eligibility of the remitter/beneficiary category.

12.2 Where a transaction falls outside the standard permissible purpose list, the matter shall be referred to Compliance for a determination on whether the transaction may be processed, and if so, under what conditions.

12.3 For certain categories of cross-border transactions processed on Orion's behalf by its correspondent banking partners, the correspondent bank is responsible for confirming compliance with applicable foreign exchange requirements at its end, and Orion's operational checks under this Section are limited to the information visible within its own systems.

## 13. RBI Reporting

13.1 Orion shall submit periodic reports relating to cross-border transactions to the Reserve Bank of India and other applicable authorities, as required.

13.2 Reports shall generally be submitted within the timelines prescribed under applicable regulatory guidance; where data reconciliation with correspondent banks is pending, the report may be submitted on a best-available-data basis with corrections filed in a subsequent cycle.

13.3 The specific internal timeline for preparing draft reports ahead of the regulatory due date is determined by the Compliance team based on monthly transaction volumes and shall be communicated to Operations at the start of each reporting cycle.

## 14. Reconciliation

14.1 Cross-border transaction volumes and values shall be reconciled between Orion's core system and correspondent bank statements on a periodic basis.

14.2 Reconciliation discrepancies shall be investigated and resolved, with unresolved items escalated to Treasury and Compliance.

## 15. Rejected Transactions

15.1 Transactions rejected by the correspondent bank or beneficiary bank shall be returned to the remitter's account, net of applicable charges, within a reasonable number of business days.

15.2 The reason for rejection shall be recorded and communicated to the customer where feasible.

## 16. Refunds

16.1 Refunds arising from processing errors attributable to Orion shall be made without deduction of processing charges.

16.2 Refunds shall require supervisory approval and shall be recorded with supporting reason codes.

## 17. Exception Handling

17.1 Deviations from the standard workflows described in this Policy shall be recorded in the exception register maintained by International Banking Operations.

17.2 The exception register shall be reviewed by Compliance on a periodic basis, the frequency of which shall be determined based on transaction volumes and risk assessment.

## 18. Audit Trails and Record Retention

18.1 The cross-border transaction system shall maintain an audit trail of transaction creation, approval, modification, and cancellation actions, including the identity of the acting employee.

18.2 Transaction records, including audit trails, shall be retained for a minimum of eight years from the date of the transaction, in accordance with the Company's broader Record Retention Policy.

18.3 Notwithstanding Section 18.2, supporting purpose documentation referenced in Section 11 shall be retained for a minimum of five years from the date of the transaction, or such longer period as operationally convenient.

## 19. Roles and Responsibilities

19.1 International Banking Operations shall be responsible for day-to-day processing in accordance with this Policy.

19.2 Compliance shall be responsible for periodic monitoring, regulatory reporting oversight, and approval of exceptions involving high-value or high-risk transactions.

19.3 Treasury shall be responsible for maintaining the list of authorized banking partners and foreign exchange rate publication.

## 20. Policy Exceptions

20.1 Exceptions to this Policy shall be documented and approved by the Head of International Banking Operations or the Chief Compliance Officer, as applicable to the nature of the exception.

## 21. Document Review

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically, and updated to reflect changes in correspondent banking arrangements, regulatory expectations, and operational practice.

### Annexure A — Illustrative Thresholds (for internal reference)

| Transaction Type | Threshold | Control Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Outward remittance (retail) | Up to USD 5,000 equivalent | Single-authorization processing |
| Outward remittance (above retail) | Above USD 5,000 equivalent | Maker-checker approval |
| Outward remittance (high value) | Above USD 25,000 equivalent | Compliance Officer sign-off required |
| Inward remittance (standard) | Up to USD 10,000 equivalent | Same-day processing target |
