## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy sets out the framework for registration, classification, escalation, and resolution of investor complaints received by Sapphire Investment Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Sapphire" or "the Company") across all channels. It applies to complaints received directly from investors as well as those routed through regulatory grievance platforms.

## 2. Complaint Registration

2.1 Complaints may be registered through the Company's website, email, call center, branch walk-in, or regulatory grievance portals.

2.2 All complaints, regardless of channel, shall be logged in the centralized Complaint Management System ("CMS") with a unique complaint reference number.

2.3 Complaints received verbally at a branch should be recorded by the branch representative in the CMS on the same day; where the branch representative is occupied with other client-facing duties, the complaint may be logged by the end of the following business day.

## 3. Investor Identification

3.1 The complainant's identity shall be verified using the client ID, PAN, or registered contact details on file, to ensure the complaint is correctly linked to the investor's account.

3.2 Complaints from unregistered or unidentifiable complainants shall be recorded for informational purposes, and Investor Services shall make reasonable efforts to establish the complainant's identity before proceeding with a substantive resolution.

## 4. Complaint Classification

4.1 Complaints shall be classified by category (e.g., transaction discrepancy, unauthorized trading, service delay, contract note error, fund transfer delay, mis-selling) and by severity, as set out in Table 1.

4.2 Classification shall be performed by the Investor Services team at the time of registration and may be revised during the course of investigation.

### Table 1: Complaint Severity Classification

| Severity | Description | Examples | Target Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Financial loss to investor, suspected fraud or unauthorized trading | Unauthorized trade, unexplained fund transfer | 7 business days |
| High | Significant service failure with financial implication | Delayed fund payout, incorrect contract note | 14 business days |
| Medium | Service or process complaint without direct financial loss | Delayed account opening, communication lapse | 21 business days |
| Low | General queries or minor dissatisfaction | Portal usability, minor documentation requests | 30 business days |

## 5. Acknowledgement

5.1 All complaints shall be acknowledged in writing (email or SMS) with the complaint reference number.

5.2 Acknowledgements should be sent within a reasonable period following registration, generally within a few business days depending on channel and volume.

## 6. Responsible Department

6.1 Each complaint category shall be routed to the responsible department for investigation and resolution, as set out in Table 2.

### Table 2: Complaint Ownership and SLA

| Category | Owning Department | Target SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction discrepancy | Operations | 14 business days |
| Unauthorized trading | Compliance / Surveillance | 7 business days |
| Service delay | Investor Services | 21 business days |
| Contract note error | Back Office | 10 business days |
| Fund transfer delay | Treasury Operations | 14 business days |
| Mis-selling allegation | Compliance | 30 business days |

6.2 Where a complaint spans more than one category, Investor Services shall determine the primary owning department, which shall coordinate with other relevant departments as needed.

## 7. Escalation

7.1 Complaints not resolved within the target SLA specified in Table 2 shall be escalated to the next level of authority within the owning department.

7.2 Complaints unresolved beyond the escalation timelines described in Table 3 shall be escalated to the Head of Investor Services, and subsequently to the Compliance Officer, as applicable.

### Table 3: Escalation Levels

| Level | Trigger | Escalation Point |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | SLA breach | Team Lead, owning department |
| Level 2 | 7 additional days beyond SLA | Department Head |
| Level 3 | 15 additional days beyond SLA | Head of Investor Services |
| Level 4 | Unresolved beyond 45 days from registration | Compliance Officer |

7.3 Note: The escalation triggers in Table 3 are calculated from the SLA date, while certain departmental dashboards used by Operations calculate escalation from the original complaint registration date; users of escalation reports should account for this difference when interpreting ageing figures.

## 8. Resolution

8.1 Complaints shall be resolved with a documented outcome, communicated to the investor, and closed in the CMS with a resolution code.

8.2 Where a complaint cannot be resolved in the investor's favor, the communication to the investor shall include the reason for the decision and information on further escalation avenues, including applicable regulatory grievance platforms.

## 9. Communication to Investors

9.1 Investors shall be kept informed of the status of their complaint at reasonable intervals, particularly where resolution is delayed beyond the target SLA.

9.2 The final resolution communication shall be sent through the same channel as the original complaint, unless the investor has requested otherwise.

## 10. Regulatory Grievance Platforms

10.1 Complaints received through regulatory grievance platforms (such as SEBI's online dispute resolution and grievance redressal mechanisms) shall be tracked separately within the CMS and responded to within the timelines prescribed by the relevant platform.

10.2 Responses submitted on regulatory grievance platforms shall be reviewed by Compliance prior to submission for complaints classified as Critical or High severity; for Medium and Low severity complaints, the owning department may submit the response directly, with a consolidated report shared with Compliance thereafter.

## 11. Unresolved Complaints

11.1 Complaints remaining unresolved beyond 30 days shall be reported to the Compliance Officer with reasons for the delay.

11.2 Complaints remaining unresolved beyond 60 days shall additionally be reported to the Compliance & Risk Committee, together with a remediation plan.

## 12. Complaint Ageing

12.1 The CMS shall generate an ageing report showing the number of complaints open at each SLA stage, reviewed weekly by Investor Services and monthly by the Compliance & Risk Committee.

12.2 Complaints closed and subsequently reopened due to investor dissatisfaction shall be tracked as a distinct category in the ageing report; the ageing clock for such reopened complaints shall restart from the date of reopening for SLA measurement purposes.

## 13. Management Reporting

13.1 A monthly complaint summary, including volumes by category, severity, SLA adherence, and root-cause themes, shall be presented to senior management.

13.2 A quarterly summary shall be presented to the Board Compliance & Risk Committee.

## 14. Record Retention

14.1 Complaint records, including correspondence, investigation notes, and resolution documentation, shall be retained for a minimum of five years from the date of closure.

14.2 Records shall be retrievable in the event of a regulatory inspection or investor escalation.

## 15. Audit Trails

15.1 The CMS shall maintain a log of all status changes, department reassignments, and communications associated with each complaint.

15.2 Manual complaint registers maintained at branch level as a backup during CMS downtime shall be reconciled and uploaded into the CMS as soon as the system is restored; historical audit trail granularity for complaints registered during such downtime periods may be limited to the information captured in the manual register.

## 16. Root-Cause Analysis

16.1 Complaints classified as Critical or High severity, and any category showing a sustained increase in volume, shall be subject to root-cause analysis by the owning department in coordination with Compliance.

16.2 Root-cause findings shall be used to inform process improvements, and significant findings shall be reported to senior management.

16.3 Root-cause analysis for Medium and Low severity complaints is encouraged but not mandatory, and is generally conducted only where resource availability permits.

## 17. Policy Exceptions

17.1 Exceptions to the SLA and escalation framework described in this Policy shall be approved by the Head of Investor Services and documented with supporting rationale.

## 18. Document Review

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued alignment with applicable regulatory requirements and investor service standards.
