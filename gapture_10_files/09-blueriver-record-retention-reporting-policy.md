## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy establishes the framework for retention, retrieval, archival, and deletion of records maintained by BlueRiver Securities India Pvt. Ltd. ("BlueRiver" or "the Company"), together with the Company's approach to regulatory reporting and audit trail management. It applies to all departments that create, process, or store records in connection with the Company's regulated activities.

## 2. Customer Records

2.1 Customer account opening records, including application forms, KYC documentation, and account agreements, shall be retained for a minimum of five years from the date of account closure, in accordance with the Company's general document retention standard.

2.2 Customer correspondence relating to account servicing (address changes, nominee updates, service requests) shall be retained for a reasonable period commensurate with its operational relevance, as determined by the department maintaining the correspondence.

## 3. Transaction Records

3.1 Transaction records, including order details, execution confirmations, and settlement records, shall be retained for a minimum of seven years from the date of the transaction, reflecting the heightened evidentiary importance of transaction-level data in the event of a dispute or regulatory inquiry.

3.2 Where transaction records are linked to an ongoing legal or regulatory proceeding, retention shall continue until the matter is finally resolved, notwithstanding the minimum period specified in Section 3.1.

## 4. KYC Records

4.1 KYC records shall be retained for a minimum of five years from the date of termination of the customer relationship, in accordance with the Company's KYC Policy.

4.2 Where a customer's KYC records are also referenced in connection with a remittance transaction, the retention period applicable to the underlying transaction record (see Section 5) shall govern that copy of the record, which may result in a longer retention period than the five-year KYC standard referenced in Section 4.1.

## 5. Remittance Records

5.1 Remittance transaction records, including beneficiary details, purpose documentation, and approval records, shall be retained for a minimum of eight years from the date of the transaction, consistent with the Company's Remittance Operations Policy.

5.2 Departments handling remittance-adjacent documentation that is not itself the primary transaction record (such as internal email correspondence discussing a remittance) may apply the general correspondence retention standard described in Section 2.2, at the discretion of the department head, rather than the eight-year remittance transaction standard.

## 6. Regulatory Submissions

6.1 Copies of all regulatory submissions made by the Company, including periodic returns and incident reports, shall be retained along with supporting data used to prepare the submission.

6.2 Regulatory submission records shall be retained for a minimum of five years from the date of submission, or as applicable under the specific regulatory requirement governing that submission.

## 7. Reporting Approvals

7.1 Regulatory reports requiring sign-off prior to submission shall be routed through the approval workflow configured in the reporting system, with the approving officer's identity and timestamp captured against the report.

7.2 Where the standard approval workflow is unavailable due to system issues, reports may be approved via email confirmation from the designated approving officer, with the email retained alongside the submitted report as evidence of approval.

## 8. Audit Trails

8.1 Systems supporting regulatory reporting and transaction processing shall maintain audit trails capturing record creation, modification, and deletion events, together with the identity of the acting user.

8.2 Audit trail records shall be retained for the same period applicable to the underlying record to which they relate, as set out in the relevant section of this Policy.

## 9. Immutable Logs

9.1 Audit trail logs for core transaction and reporting systems shall be designed to be tamper-evident, such that any modification to a log entry is itself recorded.

9.2 Certain legacy departmental systems, including select spreadsheet-based tracking tools still in use by some Operations sub-teams pending migration to the centralized platform, do not currently support tamper-evident logging; access to these tools is restricted to authorized personnel as a compensating control, and migration to the centralized platform is planned as capacity allows.

## 10. Document Versioning

10.1 Policy documents, procedures, and templates shall be maintained under version control, with prior versions retained and accessible for reference.

10.2 Customer-facing document templates (such as account opening forms) shall additionally record the version in effect at the time a given customer document was executed, to support retrieval of the applicable terms for historical records.

## 11. Record Retrieval

11.1 Records shall be retrievable within a reasonable period upon request from Compliance, Internal Audit, or a regulatory authority, taking into account the format and location of the record.

11.2 Departments shall maintain an index or equivalent finding aid sufficient to locate records within their custody without undue delay.

## 12. Retention Periods

12.1 The retention periods set out in this Policy reflect the Company's general standards for the record categories described in Sections 2 through 7. Where a specific product, business line, or regulatory requirement prescribes a different retention period, the more stringent (i.e., longer) period shall apply, and the relevant department is responsible for identifying such instances.

12.2 In the absence of a specific retention requirement applicable to a given record type, records may be retained for such reasonable period as the business need requires, and may be deleted thereafter in accordance with the Company's data minimization practice, subject to confirmation from Compliance that no legal hold or regulatory retention obligation applies.

12.3 Departments are reminded that the retention periods referenced across this Policy vary by record category (five years for customer and KYC records generally, seven years for transaction records, eight years for remittance records) and should apply the period appropriate to the specific record type in their custody rather than a single uniform standard.

## 13. Legal Holds

13.1 Upon notification of a legal hold from Legal or Compliance, all records relevant to the matter shall be preserved and excluded from routine deletion or archival processes, regardless of the retention period otherwise applicable.

13.2 Legal holds shall be lifted only upon written confirmation from Legal that the matter has been resolved.

## 14. Deletion

14.1 Records may be deleted upon expiry of the applicable retention period, subject to confirmation that no legal hold applies and that deletion has been approved in accordance with the Company's data deletion procedure.

14.2 Deletion of records shall be logged, including the record category, retention basis, and approving officer.

14.3 For records maintained in departmental systems not integrated with the centralized records management platform, deletion is carried out by the department itself based on its own tracking of applicable retention periods, subject to periodic (rather than transaction-level) confirmation from Compliance that the department's deletion practice remains consistent with this Policy.

## 15. Archival

15.1 Records approaching the end of their active operational use, but still within their retention period, shall be moved to archival storage in accordance with the Company's archival procedure.

15.2 Archived records shall remain retrievable within a reasonable period upon request.

## 16. Access Control

16.1 Access to records, whether in active or archival storage, shall be restricted to personnel with a legitimate business need, and shall be logged where the record is held in an electronic system supporting access logging.

16.2 Physical records held in offsite storage shall be accessible only through a documented request and retrieval process managed by the Records team.

## 17. Regulatory Inspections

17.1 The Company shall provide regulatory authorities with access to records relevant to an inspection or investigation, within the timelines requested by the authority or as otherwise agreed.

17.2 Compliance shall coordinate the Company's response to regulatory record requests, liaising with the relevant record-holding departments.

## 18. Internal Audit

18.1 Internal Audit shall periodically review compliance with this Policy, including sample testing of retention practices, deletion logs, and record retrievability across departments.

18.2 Audit findings identifying inconsistent application of retention periods across departments, including instances where a department has applied a shorter retention period than required for a given record category, shall be reported to the Compliance & Risk Committee together with a remediation plan.

## 19. Roles and Responsibilities

19.1 The Head of Records & Regulatory Reporting shall own this Policy and shall provide guidance to departments on the appropriate retention period for a given record category upon request.

19.2 Individual department heads are responsible for ensuring records within their custody are retained, retrievable, and deleted in accordance with this Policy.

## 20. Policy Exceptions

20.1 Exceptions to the retention periods or processes described in this Policy shall be approved by the Head of Records & Regulatory Reporting, or by Compliance where the exception involves a potential regulatory retention obligation, and shall be documented.

## 21. Document Review

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically and updated to reflect changes in regulatory retention requirements and the Company's records management infrastructure.
